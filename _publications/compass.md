title: "Compass: A Multi-View Framework for Prostate Cancer Detection from Rotational Micro-Ultrasound"
collection: publications
excerpt: 'Does analyzing single micro-ultrasound frames capture enough context for reliable prostate cancer detection, or is the full 3D picture necessary? In this work, we introduce Compass, a framework that models entire micro-ultrasound studies as streams of rotationally-linked images, fusing multi-view sweep data with biopsy frames through a transformer conditioned on probe angle. Evaluated across two clinical centers, Compass outperforms frame-based, multi-instance learning, and video model baselines at the patient level, with ablations confirming that sweep context, angle embeddings, and cross-branch reasoning each contribute to its gains.'
