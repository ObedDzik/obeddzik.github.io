title: "Learning Where to Look: A Reinforcement Learning Framework for Robust Micro-Ultrasound Prostate Cancer Detection"
collection: publications
excerpt: 'Accurate detection of suspicious tissue in micro-ultrasound depends heavily on clinical experience, and weak, core-level labels with class imbalance make training deep models for this task difficult. In this work, we introduce Prost-RL, a framework that integrates a lightweight reinforcement learning policy into a foundation-model encoder-decoder, learning where to look before decoding and producing interpretable spatial attention maps that guide localization and classification. Trained with a noise-robust objective and refined via ranking rewards on a multi-center dataset of 6,607 biopsy cores from 693 patients, Prost-RL outperforms state-of-the-art methods, reaching 79.0 AUROC for core detection and 79.3 AUROC for significant cancer classification, and improves sensitivity at 80% specificity by 4.5 points over the strongest baseline.'
